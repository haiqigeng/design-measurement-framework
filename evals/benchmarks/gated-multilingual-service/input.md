# Fixed evidence package: gated multilingual service portal

Create a whole-service, as-is measurement framework for the fictional Civic
Service Portal. Production scope contains customer and provider secure areas
in French, Dutch, and German. Production may be inspected only through safe,
reversible navigation. A representative UAT environment is authorized for
synthetic authenticated testing.

Direct UAT observation succeeds for the provider role and exposes dashboard
triage, case submission, worker administration, remittance review, and billing
history. Customer authentication returns an identity-provider error before the
secure area opens. Record that attempted boundary accurately.

The supplied current technical route inventory identifies customer secure-area
families for creating a service request, checking account balance, reviewing
transaction history, and recovering or cancelling an eligible request. It also
confirms provider route families for case submission, worker administration,
remittance review, and billing history. The route inventory proves that these
capabilities exist; it does not prove rendered execution or successful
completion.

The business brief states that customers should complete eligible self-service
tasks without assisted support, providers should resolve operational work
accurately and promptly, and authentication or processing failures must not be
hidden by higher activity volume. Backend confirmation is required for a
completed request or resolved provider case. No corporate North Star or target
has been approved.

The same UAT origin represents more than one production locale. No evidence of
pixel-identical locale parity is supplied. Preserve the production scope,
distinguish direct observation from technical confirmation, and make any
environment or locale extrapolation explicit. Do not create a tracking plan,
GA4 event list, GTM design, dashboard, or target.
