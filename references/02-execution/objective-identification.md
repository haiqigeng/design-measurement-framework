## Treat Inferred Objectives As Hypotheses

Use website and supporting evidence to infer plausible digital objectives. Do
not claim that observed journeys alone establish corporate strategy. Mark each
objective as `confirmed`, `hypothesis`, `unsupported`, or
`out_of_measurement_scope` and preserve its origin as `client_required` or
`inferred`.

Keep a client-required objective even when evidence does not support it. Mark
the tension and measurement boundary instead of silently deleting or rewriting
it.

## Build Value Streams

For every material journey, identify:

- the user's intended outcome;
- the value created for the organization or mission;
- the value created for the user or customer;
- the business-system or lifecycle outcome that confirms value;
- the principal failure or harm that should not be optimized through.

Group related outcomes into value streams. Allow a journey to support more than
one value domain. Avoid forcing every organization into revenue or lead
categories.

## Run Four Candidate Sweeps

Record one `objective_consideration` for every topic considered.

### Value-stream sweep

Consider at least one outcome objective for every material value stream and
journey family. Include revenue, qualified demand, subscription, service,
mission, engagement, retention, support, operational, or other locally
evidenced value as applicable.

### Lifecycle sweep

Consider only applicable stages, such as discovery/acquisition, activation,
engagement, conversion, fulfilment, retention, expansion, renewal, advocacy,
support, and recovery. Record `none_with_reason` or `out_of_scope` for
inapplicable stages instead of manufacturing an objective.

### Stakeholder sweep

Consider the decisions of applicable owners: marketing, product/UX, ecommerce,
sales, finance, customer success, support, operations, content/editorial,
service delivery, risk, compliance, partners, and mission/program owners.

### Risk and guardrail sweep

Consider quality, failure, customer harm, cost, cancellation, refund, complaint,
unsubscribe, support escalation, fulfilment, accessibility, or service-quality
outcomes that could degrade while a growth metric improves.

## Resolve Candidates

Use these resolutions:

- `objective_proposed` when a distinct objective is retained;
- `covered_by_existing` when an existing objective faithfully covers the topic;
- `none_with_reason` when evidence does not justify an objective;
- `out_of_scope` when the topic is outside the declared framework;
- `unresolved` only with a named exception.

Merge restatements. Preserve one primary objective per coherent value stream
when the evidence supports prioritization; do not force one global primary
objective across unrelated business models.

## Specify Each Objective

Record:

- stable ID and statement;
- value stream;
- origin and evidence status;
- priority within its value stream;
- linked journeys;
- rationale and evidence references;
- confidence;
- likely owner role when inferable;
- assumptions or conflicts.

Phrase inferred objectives in the organization's own vocabulary. Prefer a
specific outcome and population over generic phrases such as “increase
engagement.”

## Appropriateness Tests

Retain an objective only when it:

- is supported by local evidence or explicitly required by the client;
- describes an outcome rather than an implementation action;
- is distinct from other objectives;
- is relevant to the declared scope and target state;
- is at least partly assessable through digital or linked business-system data;
- fits the organization's observed model and maturity;
- can support at least one meaningful decision.

Keep a purely offline or unsupported client objective visible with its status;
do not pretend that the web framework can measure it.
