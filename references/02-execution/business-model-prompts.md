# Business-Model Prompts

Use only the applicable sections. Generate candidates, then require local
evidence and analyst judgment before retaining any journey, objective, KPI, or
requirement.

## Ecommerce And Transactional Retail

Consider discovery, search/listing, product evaluation, availability, cart,
checkout, payment, purchase confirmation, fulfilment, account, reorder, return,
refund, cancellation, loyalty, support, and promotion journeys. Consider gross
and net revenue, transaction and item conversion, order value, checkout
progression, payment failure, cancellation/return/refund rates, fulfilment
quality, repeat purchase, and margin dependencies.

## Lead Generation And Long-Cycle Sales

Consider content or product discovery, eligibility, configurator, contact,
quote, booking, callback, catalogue, application, confirmation, CRM
qualification, sales acceptance, outcome, rejection, and nurture/re-entry.
Consider qualified demand rather than form volume alone. Pair lead growth with
quality, rejection, response-time, cancellation, or unsubscribe guardrails.

## SaaS And Subscription

Consider acquisition, signup, trial, activation, feature adoption, habit,
upgrade, downgrade, renewal, failed payment, pause, cancellation, churn,
reactivation, account administration, and support. Define cohorts and time
windows explicitly. Pair activation or expansion with retention, failure,
support, and churn guardrails.

## Content And Media

Consider discovery, search, article/video/audio consumption, recirculation,
subscription, registration, newsletter, ad or sponsor value, return behavior,
content completion, and contribution. Distinguish raw views from qualified
consumption and downstream value. Pair engagement with satisfaction,
subscription quality, bounce-back, or ad-load effects when evidenced.

## Marketplace Or Multi-Sided Platform

Consider both or all participant sides, supply creation, demand discovery,
matching, transaction/contact, fulfilment, trust, disputes, repeat activity,
liquidity, and balance. Avoid one-sided north stars that hide degradation for
another participant group.

## Service, Support, And Self-Service

Consider issue discovery, diagnostic content, eligibility, self-service task,
contact escalation, resolution, repeat contact, account recovery, outage,
complaint, and satisfaction. Do not equate lower contact volume with successful
deflection without resolution or quality evidence.

## Public Service, Nonprofit, Education, And Mission Sites

Consider service discovery, eligibility, application, completion, access,
inclusion, donation, volunteering, resource use, learning progress, mission
outcome, support, and failure to access. Use mission and user-value outcomes;
do not force commercial revenue, lead, or advocacy stages when they do not fit.

## Informational Or Corporate Sites

Consider audience intent, content discovery, high-value content consumption,
investor/candidate/partner journeys, document access, contact, service
navigation, trust, and onward action. Avoid treating pageviews or generic
engagement as primary success without an evidenced outcome.
